\section{Research Statement}

\ \ \ \ My research centers on \textbf{algorithm--infrastructure co-design} for LLMs and agents. I started with \textbf{Infra4AI}, first building inference infrastructure (e.g., EPIC, DeepServe, RedKnot, Hy4, MiMo-V2) and then agent harnesses and memory systems (e.g., PenguinHarness, Akashic). As AI grew more capable than I am at building infrastructure, I chose to build the AI that replaces me rather than compete with it. Therefore, I now focus on \textbf{AI4Infra}, specifically \textbf{RSI (recursive self-improvement) for agent harnesses}: training models to write and evolve their own harnesses (e.g., GDPevo, Awesome-RSI). Next, I may extend AI4Infra to inference and training infrastructure.

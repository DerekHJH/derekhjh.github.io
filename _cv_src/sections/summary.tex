\section{Research Summary}

\ \ \ \ My research centers on building high-performance Agentic systems, aimed at maximizing both system \textbf{efficiency} and \textbf{capabilities} through the principle of algorithm-infrastructure co-design. 

\ \ \ \ In the initial stage of my doctoral studies, I trained small-to-median-sized deep learning models to optimize adaptive compilation and code generation (ASE'23, ESEC/FSE'23). Subsequently, my focus shifted to agentic/LLM infrastructure, where I proposed novel mechanisms for Position-Independent Caching and Reasoning-Aware Attention Sparsity---embodied in systems such as EPIC (ICML'25), CacheSlide (FAST'26), RaaS (ACL'25), and Lil (ACL'26)---which significantly enhance inference throughput and memory utilization. 

\ \ \ \ My academic research is deeply integrated with industrial practice; at Huawei Cloud, I was deeply engaged in the development of the serverless LLM serving system DeepServe (ATC'25); at Xiaomi, I co-led the co-design of model architecture and agent memory project, contributing critical optimizations to the MiMo-V2 (Flash/Pro) foundation model and agentic systems. As the chief scientist of the AI startup PrismShadow, I developed GDPevo, a benchmark for evaluating self-evolving agents.


\ \ \ \ Looking ahead, I aim to investigate \textbf{agent self-evolution and recursive self-improvement (RSI)}, with a focus on enabling agents to \textbf{efficiently} refine their reasoning, memory, and learning strategies through continual interaction and experience.

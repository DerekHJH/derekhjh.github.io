Hooray~ you caught me! I am Junhao Hu, a fifth-year Ph.D. student at [School of Computer Science, Peking University](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. [Tao Xie](https://taoxiease.github.io/). And I am lucky to work with and advised by Prof. [Chenxi Wang](https://wangchenxi7.github.io/home/), Prof. [Yizhou Shan](http://lastweek.io/), [Hailin Zhang](https://hugozhl.github.io/), [Kechi Zhang](https://zkcpku.github.io/), and [Xusheng Chen](https://michaelxschen.github.io/).

I am currently an RSI researcher at Tencent Hunyuan, working on **RSI for harness and infrastructure**. Feel free to [reach out](mailto:junhaohu@stu.pku.edu.cn) for discussion or collaboration!

I got my bachelor's degree from [Kuang Yaming Honors School, Nanjing University](https://dii.nju.edu.cn/) in 2022, where I did research on Compilation Resource Prediction, advised by Prof. [Yanyan Jiang](https://jyywiki.cn/).

My research centers on **algorithm–infrastructure co-design** for LLMs and agents. I started with **Infra4AI**: optimizing inference infrastructure through serving systems, model–infra co-design, sparse attention, and position-independent caching (e.g., EPIC, DeepServe, RedKnot, Hy4, MiMo-V2). Then, as agents grew more powerful, I began building agent harnesses and memory systems (e.g., PenguinHarness, Akashic). Later, as AI grew more capable, building infrastructure by hand is no longer enough: rather than compete with AI, I train AI to build better infrastructure (**AI4Infra**). I now work on **RSI for harness**: training models to absorb harness capabilities such as compaction, skills, and self-evolution, and to write and evolve harnesses themselves (e.g., GDPevo, Awesome-RSI). Next, I plan to extend AI4Infra to inference infrastructure and even training infrastructure.
